About Me

I'm a B.S. Mechanical Engineering student at Georgia Tech, pursuing a Robotics Minor and in the John H. Martinson Honors Program. My expected graduation date is December 2028.

As someone who has always been eager to create things, I'm excited to pair extensive extracurricular enrichment with technical coursework to contribute to an engineering team and find innovative solutions to complex challenges that better the world around us. I'm experienced in taking projects through the engineering process from conception to release, and leading teams through strong communication and effective resource management and prioritization.

At Tech, my main focus is working on the suspension subteam for HyTech Racing, our student-run Formula SAE Electric team. I also build for RoboJackets BattleBots and serve on the ASME @ GT Volunteer Board. Prior to this, I worked as a Mechanical Engineering Intern at REV Robotics for the summers of 2024, 2025, and 2026. I was also a student in FIRST Robotics for 10 seasons, competing across FIRST Lego League, FIRST Tech Challenge, and FIRST Robotics Competition in various roles including Team Captain, Mechanical Design Lead, Software Lead, and Robot Driver.

I love making new creations and learning new skills, and can't wait to continue doing so!
